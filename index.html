Enroll a family.

Five minutes at the visit. Nothing after that.

Who is eligible. Any child under age 3 in a family that meets WIC income guidelines (185% FPL). One enrollment per child. Families already using another diaper bank are still eligible.

The steps

  1. Open your site's enrollment link on your tablet or computer.
  2. Hand it to the family or walk them through it. The form takes about 5 minutes and includes 3 short evaluation questions.
  3. The family completes the form and agrees to the terms and conditions. If you are assisting, check the staff attestation box.
  4. The family submits. They get a text with a verification link right away.
  5. Done. Nothing else is needed from you.

What to tell the family about what happens next

They will get a text with a verification link right away, and they need to click it. Within one business day of clicking, their eCard arrives by text. Their 6 month benefit starts when the card is issued. Everything after that, including retailer instructions and the support contact, is texted to them.

Why the evaluation questions are on the form

Families may ask why the form has extra questions. Here is what to tell them, depending on your site type. Individual responses are never shared with your site.

WIC clinics

The form asks whether the family has ever gotten free diapers from a local organization, what made it hard if they have, and whether a lack of diapers has caused them to miss work, skip changes, or cut back on other essentials. These help the state document what diaper access looks like in counties with no diaper bank.

Home visiting programs

The form asks whether the family had connected to WIC, Medicaid, SNAP, or childcare assistance before this visit, whether they had a plan for getting diapers, and how concerned they are about affording diapers in the coming months. These help the state understand whether home visiting is an effective way to connect families to this benefit.

How the program works.

Reference information for answering family questions. Each section is short. If you need more, check the FAQ.

The benefit

$40 per month per child, loaded onto a digital eCard. Up to 3 children per household, so $120 per month at most. It lasts 6 months from the date the eCard is issued, with no renewal. This is a limited pilot for State Fiscal Year 2027 and services beyond this year are not guaranteed.

Where the card works

Walmart, Albertsons (Jewel-Osco), Kroger stores (Food 4 Less, Mariano's), Walgreens, CVS, Dollar General, Family Dollar, and more. The card runs on the OTC network, which these retailers already accept. Families can use it at the register, at self checkout, or online depending on the store. Retailer specific instructions are texted to families with their card.

Eligibility

Child under age 3. Family meets WIC income guidelines (185% FPL). If they are eligible for WIC, they qualify. Home visiting sites can also use Medicaid eligibility. One enrollment per child, no duplicates across sites. Families already receiving diapers from another program are still eligible.

Timeline

Enrollment runs October 1 through December 23, 2026. That is when your staff's involvement happens. After December 23, families keep receiving their benefit through the end of their 6 month window, which runs into June 2027 for December enrollees. Your staff are not involved after enrollment closes.

What you are not responsible for

No physical diapers. No warehouse, no pallets, no volunteers. No income verification beyond what you already do for WIC. No managing the eCard or fielding benefit questions. Once a family is enrolled, the Diaper Dollars support team handles everything. Families get the support number and email with their card.

About the Diaper Distribution Pilot Program.

A state funded test of whether Illinois can put a monthly diaper benefit in families' hands through the systems they already use.

What it is

The Diaper Distribution Pilot Program was created by the Illinois General Assembly to test whether Illinois can provide a monthly diaper benefit to families with young children. It is funded through the Illinois Department of Human Services and the Illinois Department of Public Health, and operated by MOD Collective through the Diaper Dollars platform.

Why it exists

No federal program covers diapers. SNAP, WIC, TANF, and Medicaid all exclude them. A month's supply costs $70 to $100 per child, and one in two families with young children cannot consistently afford the diapers they need. When families run short, children stay in wet diapers longer, parents miss work because childcare requires a daily diaper supply, and families cut back on food or medicine to cover the gap.

How it works

Instead of distributing physical diapers, the program loads a monthly benefit onto a digital eCard that families use at retailers they already shop at. That means no warehouses, no volunteer coordination, and no transportation logistics. Families choose their own brand and size. The model was built to work through existing enrollment channels like WIC clinics and home visiting programs, so the state does not have to build new infrastructure.

What previous years have shown

The program has operated across multiple regions in Illinois. Over 92% of enrolled families report reducing or eliminating their need for emergency diaper sources after enrolling. The eCard model has launched at new sites quickly and with minimal disruption to existing workflows.

What this year is testing

FY27 is focused on two questions. First, can the program serve as the primary diaper resource in counties that have no diaper bank or organized diaper assistance at all? Every WIC site in this year's program is in a county identified as having zero existing diaper distribution. Second, is a WIC clinic the best way to reach families, or can home visiting programs enroll families just as effectively? Two home visiting programs are participating alongside the WIC sites so the state can compare enrollment, utilization, and reach across both models.

The answers will inform whether and how the program expands in future years.

Frequently asked questions

Do we enroll babies only, or can we enroll toddlers too?

Any child under age 3. Newborns, infants, and toddlers all qualify.

Is there a cap on how many families we can enroll?

No per site cap. The 5,500 target is shared across all 12 sites, first come first served. Enroll every eligible family you see.

What if a family already gets diapers from another program?

They are still eligible. The only restriction is no duplicate enrollments for the same child across sites.

Do we need a Release of Information form?

No. The family consents to share their information directly on the enrollment form. Nothing separate is needed from your office.

Are there printed materials?

No. Everything is texted to families.

What if a family does not get their verification text?

Have them check the phone number they entered. If it is correct and they still have not received it within a few minutes, direct them to the support contact above.

How long does the enrollment form take?

About 5 minutes, including the evaluation questions.
